6 · The trust game — how the video testimonials actually work

You spotted the videos; here's the full system (complete inventory: findings/trust-game.md):

  • All videos are self-hosted MP4s — zero YouTube embeds. The flagship is one file: client-testimonial.mp4 — a single multi-client reel ("Words from Our C-Suite Partners") in a lightbox on the homepage. Flagship case studies (KFC, Adidas) each get one custom hero/results video. Self-hosting = no YouTube recommendations leaking your visitor to competitors, no cookie banners, full control.
  • Written proof is componentized: 8 named, titled client quotes (Neeraj Tiwari of Americana, Simon Wing of Edfundo…) built once and reused across three contexts — homepage slider, exit-intent modal, per-case-study carousel.
  • Numbers make it credible: per-case metric tiles (KFC: 22% conversion increase, 50%+ orders via app, 60%+ repeat purchases) and a sitewide stats bar (10+ years, 1700+ specialists, 35+ industries).
  • Verification anchors: a live Clutch rating widget linked to their real profile in every footer + the Deloitte Fast 50 badge — the two elements repeated sitewide, because they're independently checkable.
  • Distribution is layered, not a testimonials page: sitewide chrome carries the baseline (Clutch + Deloitte + exit modal); the homepage carries hero-grade assets (awards carousel, the video reel); each case study carries its own specific proof; service pages borrow proof via inline linked client mentions.
Your 10-step replication plan (for your 10–12 clients)

1. Film one multi-client flagship reel first — not twelve separate videos; self-host the MP4. 2. Collect quote + headshot + real name/title from every client, no exceptions. 3. Build one reusable "trust card" component; deploy it in three contexts (homepage slider, exit modal, case pages). 4. Force ≥2 concrete metrics per client ("71% invoice-processing time cut"), no vague praise. 5. Deep case-study pages for your 3–4 strongest clients only. 6. Cross-link every case study ↔ its service page, both directions. 7. Get real Clutch/G2 reviews before displaying any badge. 8. Stats bar with defensible numbers only. 9. Exit-intent module that reuses assets from steps 1–4. 10. Build order: quotes → component → reel → 3 case studies → reviews → stats bar.
