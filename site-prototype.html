Process

How a build actually runs

  1. Week 0, process audit. We map the workflow and tell you which parts shouldn't be automated.
  2. Weeks 1–2. PoC on real data. Not a demo. Your documents, your edge cases.
  3. Weeks 3–8, build. Tools, memory, guardrails, human-in-the-loop points, eval suite.
  4. Weeks 9–12, production hardening. Monitoring, fallbacks, cost controls, handover.
  5. Ongoing, managed operation. Evals re-run weekly; drift and cost tracked.

The problem

Nine people reading email for a living

Northwind's first-notice-of-loss queue arrived as unstructured email: some with PDFs, some with photos in the body, some forwarded three times from a broker. Nine coordinators across two shifts read each one, decided severity, keyed 14 fields into the claims system and routed it. Median time from arrival to assignment was 4.2 hours; overnight arrivals waited until morning.

They had already tried a rules-based extraction tool. It handled the two broker formats it was configured for and failed on everything else, which was 40% of volume.

What we built

The architecture

[ARCHITECTURE DIAGRAM: email ingest → classifier → extraction agent → confidence gate → claims system / human queue]
  • Ingest + classifier. Decides whether an email is an FNOL at all, and what document types are attached.
  • Extraction agent. Pulls 14 fields from any format including photographs of handwritten forms, with per-field confidence.
  • Confidence gate. Anything below threshold on any critical field goes to a human: with the agent's reasoning attached, not just a flag.
  • Severity router. Applies Northwind's own triage rules, which we encoded with their claims lead in week two.
  • Audit trail. Every action, prompt and decision logged for the regulator.

The three costs

Build, run, maintain

Build

Scoping, PoC on real data, engineering, evals, hardening. Driven by process complexity and how many systems the agent must touch.

Run

Token spend per item processed, the number almost nobody publishes. Varies 10× by whether you're extracting from a page or reasoning over a corpus.

Maintain

Weekly evals, drift monitoring, prompt and tool updates as your systems change. The line item that separates a live agent from an abandoned one.
