Before you forward anything

The seven audits name real companies and describe their link-buying, fake authorship and invented star ratings. Every claim was checked against Ahrefs and live crawls, but that is research for internal use: it reads very differently published to the open web than it does in a strategy folder.

This site carries noindex and a robots.txt that disallows every crawler, so search engines skip it. The repository is public, though, so treat these URLs as private-by-obscurity rather than actually private. Share the roadmap freely; keep the audits inside the team.
