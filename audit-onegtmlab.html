4 · The backlink profile, and a correction to our own published work

Corrected 3 Aug 2026, after Jainendra challenged the finding

An earlier version of this audit, and our audits of four competitors, described the SEOExpress network as links those sites bought. Jainendra was certain we had never bought a link, which prompted a re-test. He was right, and the original reading was wrong on every site.

The test: a purchased link is dofollow. Passing ranking equity is the entire product; a nofollow link passes nothing, so there is nothing to sell. We pulled the dofollow count for every spam anchor group on all five domains.

SiteSpam anchor groupsLinksDofollowVerdict
onegtmlab.com (ours)563402sprayed, not bought
searchable.com572,3410sprayed, not bought

genesysgrowth.com (SEOExpress anchor)12430sprayed, not bought
appwrk.com (Telegram anchor)15760sprayed, not bought

Zero dofollow, on every spun-testimonial cluster, on every site. Nobody bought these. A link vendor is spraying fabricated testimonials for itself and using real domains as filler. We have corrected the searchable, omnibound, appwrk, genesys and SEO-From-Zero documents accordingly.

The anchor text reads "Before I found SEOExpress.org, I had nearly given up on improving onegtmlab.com's presence online", which is the vendor's template with our domain injected into it. It is nofollow. It arrived 17 April 2026, part of a burst of 38 in that month.

Six of the referring domains share the campaign ID bt8c9da in their URL slugs (linkrankpro.shop, rankxlinks.shop, ranklinkerpro.shop, seorankflow.shop, buyseobacklinks.shop, rankshiftseo.shop). One operator, one batch, sprayed across many targets. All of them return a 301 or 302 today.

What is genuinely dofollow and pointing at us: one link. reo.dev/blog/developer-marketing-resources-2026, DR 40, anchor "OneGTMLab". That is a real editorial mention. Of 100 live backlinks sampled, 99 are nofollow.

The action, unchanged

Disavow the cluster in Search Console anyway. It passes nothing (DR 2.3 across 365 domains proves that), removal costs nothing, and it keeps the profile clean. But this is hygiene, not remediation, and no one on the team did anything wrong here.

8 · Remaining gap list

  • HighNo llms.txt, no /ai-info, both 404. We recommend both in our own roadmap's machine layer.
  • HighOne services page against 797 tool pages. The pages that convert are outnumbered 100:1 by pages that convert nothing.
  • MediumNo Article schema on the blog, the site's strongest asset.
  • MediumNo security headers: no CSP, X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy or Permissions-Policy. Not a ranking factor, but an enterprise security review will ask.
  • MediumSitemap served as application/rss+xml rather than application/xml.
  • MediumTool slugs carry hash suffixes: /tools/lemlist-c6c2e, /tools/folderly-5954a. Clean these and merge whatever duplicates sit behind them.
The summary for the meeting

The team built 871 pages, seven genuinely strong blog posts and a working Webflow CMS. Then 846 of those pages shipped with one title tag, no canonical, no meta description and Lorem ipsum in the navigation, and the category templates were never bound to their own data.

None of that is a strategy failure and none of it needs a rebuild. Day 1 is six template fields. 2,442 searches a month sit at KD 0 to 10 with the content already written for them.

And on the backlinks: we were wrong and Jainendra was right. Nobody bought anything. That correction has been pushed to all five audits.

Method: Ahrefs API v3 (domain-rating, metrics, metrics-history, backlinks-stats, refdomains, anchors with dofollow counts, all-backlinks with source URLs, organic-keywords, keywords-explorer), full parse of all 871 sitemap URLs, direct crawl of 24 pages spanning all nine page types, five-user-agent SHA-256 comparison on three pages, pairwise body-similarity via difflib within each page type, and a live field-by-field comparison against n8n, Zapier and Lindy. Pulled 3 Aug 2026. Ahrefs traffic figures are estimates; the zero here is a genuine absence of ranking data, not a small number rounded down.
