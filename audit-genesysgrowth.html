Correction and re-test, 3 Aug 2026

An earlier version of this audit described the spam links pointing at this domain as bought. That was wrong. Every link in those clusters is nofollow, and a purchased link is dofollow because passing equity is the entire product. What this actually is: a link vendor spraying fabricated testimonials for itself and using real domains as filler. genesysgrowth.com was targeted, not complicit. The same network points at our own site, which is what prompted the re-test.

This does not change why the traffic fell. We tested the alternative directly: if links had caused it, the link profile would have shrunk. Traffic fell from 1,021 to 504 (−51%) while referring domains rose from 1,011 to 1,408 (+39%). Links are ruled out as the mechanism. The cause was content quality, exactly as this audit describes below, and that finding stands unchanged.

How to read this map. The three fatal mismatches it shows:

5 · Your keyword question: does "best AI development agency in 2026 June" beat the big players?

Short answer: no: that's the exact trap Genesys fell into, and now you can name it. Adding "June" creates a phrasing with ~zero search volume; you'd rank #1 in an empty room (2.6 visits/keyword is what that strategy yields at scale). Here's the honest mechanics of date-modified keywords:

  • Year modifiers are real: "best ai development agency 2026" has genuine volume, because searchers add the year to force fresh results. Put the year in your title, refresh the page (content + date) each January, keep the URL year-free (/best-ai-automation-agencies, never /best-ai-automation-agencies-2026) so the URL keeps its accumulated authority forever.
  • Month modifiers are noise: almost nobody searches "…June 2026", and Google treats the page as targeting the same query anyway. You gain nothing and look templated.
  • How you actually beat bigger players on a young domain: not by out-phrasing them on the head term, but by (1) down-market specificity with real volume: "best AI automation agency for insurance", "n8n automation agency". Where incumbents have no dedicated page; (2) genuinely better pages (real screenshots, real data, real methodology, everything Genesys faked); (3) the AI-search lane, where being the clearest, best-structured answer wins citations regardless of domain age. Then, as authority compounds, you climb toward the head terms.

  1. The stats linkable-asset series. Their only real link engine (215 refdomains on one post) AND their top traffic (60% of all visits from ~10 posts). One format doing both jobs. Copy it, add original client data and charts.
  2. The GEO layer, /ai-info + llms.txt + AI-crawler welcomes + zero cloaking. Best implementation we've audited; full copy-playbook in the course, Part 3.
  3. Commercial transparency: published pricing, named clients with quotes, 1-click booking. The trust layer a real business deserves, which makes the manufactured shell around it such a waste.
